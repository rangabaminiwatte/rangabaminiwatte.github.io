I'm a PhD candidate in Biomedical Data Science and Informatics at Clemson University and the Medical University of South Carolina. I'm an applied machine learning researcher working on AI methods that support biomedical discovery and clinical interpretation, with a focus on rare genetic disorders.

Research interests
======
* **Next-generation phenotyping.** Learning a quantifiable ontology of facial features from clinical images and anatomical priors, to support rare disease diagnosis.
* **Biomedical knowledge graphs.** Graph contrastive learning over multi-relational knowledge graphs to model disease similarity for patient retrieval and phenotype-based cohort discovery ([PhenoGnet](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.14037)).
* **Interpretable, multimodal diagnosis.** Combining images, clinical features and formal biological knowledge such as the Human Phenotype Ontology into predictions clinicians can inspect.

I build end-to-end deep learning pipelines across graph learning and computer vision, from data preprocessing and GPU-optimised training to evaluation and reporting. I also serve as a peer reviewer for computational genomics manuscripts.

Before Clemson, I earned a BSc in Applied Mathematics and Computer Science at the Higher School of Economics in Moscow, where I worked as a research assistant on machine learning for eye-tracking data and deep generative models for particle detector simulation.
