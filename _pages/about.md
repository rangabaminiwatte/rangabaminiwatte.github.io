Hi, I'm Ranga, a PhD candidate in Biomedical Data Science and Informatics at [Clemson University](https://www.clemson.edu/) and the Medical University of South Carolina([MUSC](https://www.musc.edu/)). I’m currently being advised by [Dr. Aaron J. Masino](https://amasino.people.clemson.edu/).

I’m passionate about developing AI and data science solutions to address complex biomedical problems. My current research focuses on learning representations of multimodal data to support rare disease diagnosis and discovery. 

Specifically, I work on (1) graph representation learning to model omics data (2) developing explainable, disease-agnostic representations of facial images that capture dysmorphic features. I’m also exploring how these representations can be combined to support disease diagnosis and the discovery of gene–disease associations.

Previously, I earned my B.Sc. in Applied Mathematics and Informatics from Higher School of Economics (HSE), Moscow, Russia. I’m a student member of Clemson University Institute for Human Genetics([CUIHG](https://scienceweb.clemson.edu/ihg/ranga-baminiwatte/)) and American Medical Informatics Association (AMIA).

I have experience in building end-to-end deep learning pipelines across graph learning and computer vision. Please reach out if you are interested in collaborating.
